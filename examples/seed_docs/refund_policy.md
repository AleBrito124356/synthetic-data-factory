# Refund Policy

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains when a
refund is available and how to request one. All amounts are refunded to the
original payment method.

## Eligibility window

You may request a full refund within 30 days of the delivery date. After 30
days and up to 60 days, purchases are eligible for store credit only. Requests
made more than 60 days after delivery are not eligible for a refund.

## Condition requirements

Physical items must be returned unused and in their original packaging.
Software licenses and downloadable products are refundable only if the license
key has not been activated. Gift cards are non-refundable.

## How to request a refund

Open a ticket from the Orders page and select "Request refund". Include the
order number and the reason for the return. Our support team reviews requests
within two business days. Once approved, refunds are processed within five to
seven business days, though the time for the funds to appear depends on your
bank.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is the result of
our error, such as a defective or incorrect item. In those cases we also cover
the return shipping cost with a prepaid label.
