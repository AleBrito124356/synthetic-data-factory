# Shipping Policy

Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders
placed before 2:00 PM local time ship the same business day; later orders ship
the next business day.

## Delivery estimates

Standard shipping arrives in three to five business days within the country.
Express shipping arrives in one to two business days. International orders take
seven to fourteen business days depending on the destination and customs
processing.

## Shipping fees

Standard shipping is free for orders over 50 dollars. Below that threshold a
flat fee of 4.99 dollars applies. Express shipping is a flat 12.99 dollars
regardless of order size. International shipping is calculated at checkout based
on weight and destination.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed as soon as the carrier scans the package. You can
also find tracking on the Orders page. If tracking has not updated after three
business days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the
carrier.

## Undeliverable packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect address,
we will contact you to confirm the address and reship it. A second shipping fee
may apply for address errors that are not our fault.
